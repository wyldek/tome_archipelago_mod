# v0.2.4 beta implementation notes

- AP characters now allow direct permanent core-stat rewards from vanilla content.
- Free/distributable stat, class, generic, category, and prodigy point pools are scrubbed continuously.
- Native post-birth talent-category unlocks and mastery changes are blocked for AP characters unless they occur inside an AP-authorized grant/evolution.
- Escort reward menus are filtered to direct core-stat increases only; talent ranks, tree/category rewards, and save bonuses are removed.
- AP +5 stat packages stack with vanilla direct stat rewards instead of treating vanilla bonuses as consuming the normal AP +50-per-stat allocation.
- Arena generic points, Sandworm Heart point rewards, Wyrm Bile category points, and similar direct point grants therefore have no spendable effect while their non-point quest/item side effects remain intact.
